# Research Statement & Academic Profile — Showrin Rahman

I completed my BSc in Computer Science at BRAC University in 2025 with a CGPA of 3.62/4.00.

## Research Overview
My research centers at the intersection of **Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and GIS / Geospatial AI**. I am particularly interested in developing scalable, data-efficient neural architectures capable of learning from high-dimensional, complex visual and multi-modal signals under real-world constraints such as severe label scarcity, sensor noise, and domain distribution shifts. My work bridges foundational architecture design (self-supervised pre-training, state-space sequence modeling, diffusion models, and adaptive attention) with empirical validation across demanding visual domains—including GIS & high-dimensional geospatial sensor arrays, multi-spectral satellite imagery, intelligent document intelligence, and controllable synthetic data generation.

## Key Research Tracks & Contributions

### 1. Computer Vision & Visual Representation Learning
- **Undergraduate Thesis (BRAC University, 2025):** Developed a self-supervised framework pairing **Masked Autoencoders (MAE)** with **Mamba State-Space Models (SSMs)** for dense semantic segmentation under label scarcity. Investigated automated segmentation of boundary structures and integrated GAN-based temporal reconstruction to handle observational gaps in time series.
- **Image Restoration & Attention (IEEE EUROCON 2025, Poland):** Investigated spatially-varying blind image deblurring using Multi-Scale Feature Fusion with Adaptive Attention (MSFAA) and the CAFI cross-attention module, achieving **+2.1 dB PSNR, +0.043 SSIM**, and an 18% reduction in computational complexity compared to state-of-the-art baselines.

### 2. GIS & Geospatial AI
- **Multi-Temporal Spatio-Temporal Analysis (IEEE ACDSA 2025):** Co-authored a multi-temporal change detection study integrating multi-spectral Sentinel-2 imagery, spectral indices (NDWI, NDVI), and supervised machine learning in Google Earth Engine to model dynamic morphological boundaries at scale.
- **SAR Satellite Image Segmentation:** Designed self-supervised deep learning workflows on Sentinel-1 SAR data for automated land-water boundary delineation and erosion detection.
- **GIS & Disaster Resilience (CrisisCrew):** Engineered a disaster management and early response platform integrating IoT sensor data, GIS mapping, and machine learning models (Hult Prize Semifinalist, OSUN Bard College Mobility).

### 3. Generative AI & Controllable Synthesis
- **Segmentation-Guided Diffusion (Springer IFIP AICT 2024, Vol. 705):** Designed TWIG, a two-step diffusion pipeline using intermediate semantic masks to decouple structural geometry from appearance, synthesizing copyright-compliant training data.
- **RL-Guided Text Layout in Diffusion Models (arXiv:2505.19291, 2025):** Introduced reinforcement-learning-based character-level layout optimization for text-to-image synthesis, improving glyph rendering fidelity and spatial visual coherence.

### 4. Intelligent Document Processing (IDP)
- **Industry R&D Collaboration (276 Holdings, Seoul):** Deployed image restoration and deep learning pipelines to production OCR systems (EasyOCR, Tesseract), applying document-specific preprocessing and MSFAA deblurring to enhance text recognition on degraded business documents.
