I completed my BSc in Computer Science at BRAC University in 2025 with a CGPA of 3.62/4.00.
My research focuses on developing deep learning and computer vision methods for remote sensing and environmental monitoring. I am particularly interested in developing scalable, high-precision approaches for automated environmental monitoring, using high-dimensional and multi-source geospatial data to extract meaningful information and detect environmentally significant and potentially hazardous open-water features.
In my undergraduate thesis, I developed a self-supervised framework combining Masked Autoencoders and Mamba for semantic segmentation of Sentinel-1 SAR imagery to detect riverbank erosion, where I explored the automated segmentation of river and land regions from SAR imagery as a foundation for detecting and monitoring riverbank erosion. I also contributed as a co-author to a study on riverbank change detection in the Jamuna River Basin, Bangladesh (IEEE ACDSA 2025), integrating Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, spectral indices, and machine learning in Google Earth Engine to analyze and predict riverbank changes.
Additionally, my research experience includes multi-scale feature fusion and adaptive attention for robust image deblurring (IEEE Eurocon 2025), where I investigated multi-scale adaptive attention for spatially varying blur, improving PSNR and SSIM while reducing computational cost. I also contributed as an independent researcher to FlowPay, an industry R&D project focused on Automatic Document Recognition and Intelligent Document Processing (IDP). I developed OCR pipelines using EasyOCR and Tesseract, implemented document-specific image preprocessing, and improved OCR accuracy through MFSAA-based image deblurring techniques. Moreover, I worked on diffusion-based image generation using segmentation masks (Springer IFIP 2025), developing a segmentation-guided diffusion approach that separates structural information from appearance to generate copyright-safe images.
These projects have strengthened my experience in deep learning, computer vision, image processing, semantic segmentation, and satellite imagery analysis, allowing me to work across both conventional computer vision and remote sensing applications.
The research environment of the Geospatial Systems and Intelligence Lab is particularly aligned with my interests because of its work on advanced AI and machine learning methods for remote sensing data and environmental applications. I am especially interested in the lab’s research on SAR and optical remote sensing of Canadian water and ice environments, including SAR image segmentation, sea and lake ice mapping, and marine oil-spill monitoring. These areas closely connect with my previous experience in SAR image segmentation and environmental monitoring while providing an opportunity for me to extend my research toward new environmental applications.
